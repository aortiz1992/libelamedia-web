Storage configurator

—

One 3D scene, turned into a configurator your customers can use.

Every module, front and handle here comes from the same file. We prepare the model, compress it for the web and build the configurator around your real catalogue: your designs, your finishes, your prices.

  • Units line up along the wall, and wall units find the free space above the low ones.
  • Fronts, frames and handles change per unit or all at once. Doors take vertical bars, drawers horizontal ones.
  • Dimensions and price update with every change, and any layout can be shared as a link.
