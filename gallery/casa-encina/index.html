Private house

Casa Encina

About the project

A long, low country house set among centuries-old holm oaks in the dehesa of Extremadura. Local granite and warm rammed earth, frameless glass and a thin concrete roof that draws one clean shadow line across the landscape.

We followed the day of the dehesa: golden afternoon light raking the stone and the earth walls, the stillness of the reflecting pool, and a night sky with no other light for miles.
